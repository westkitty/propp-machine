Generated by `npm run metrics` (`docs/metrics.json`, label `final`). Structure is measured on 1,000 tales (seeds 1–1000). Prose is measured on the 50 published tales (seeds 1–50).

- 988 of 1,000 generated sequences are distinct; sequence entropy is 9.942 bits out of a possible 9.966. At the level of whole sequences, variety is not the problem.
- **Transitions are the gap.** The conditional entropy of the next function given the current one is 0.923 bits for generated tales and 1.881 bits for the real ones (12-tale samples: 0.694 against 1.881). The generator obeys strict order, so after each function only a few successors are possible. Real tellers reorder, repeat and interleave (see ANALYSIS.md). Adding optional functions and second- and third-move shapes moved this only from 0.891 to 0.923. A trebling or interleaving generator mode is in the backlog. The real-tale figures come from 12 tales and are noisy.

## Prose (50 tales)

Three template sets: v1 = base, 2 templates per function, no alternations. v2 = adds 2–3 templates per function with [a|b] alternations and sentence openers. v3 = after measuring v2, adds short names after first mention and a wider, rarer set of openers.

Top openings. v1: dunya of (64), kuzma crookedcap (53), agafya last (49), ivashka ninth (42), tikhon who (37). v2: by then (75), so it (58), not long (57), when the (25), in the (22). v3: then the (25), when the (25), the lad (23), the count (22), in the (22).

How to read this: v1 repeated the hero's full name about 15 times per tale, and more than half its sentences recur verbatim across tales. v2 halved the repeated sentences and raised distinct-3 from 0.24 to 0.34. It also introduced three overused openers ("By then", "So it", "Not long after"). v3 fixed the openers and the name repetition and raised distinct-3 to 0.37. About a fifth of sentences still recur somewhere across the 50 tales, mostly short closers such as "Everything that had been taken was put right."

One caveat on chronology: v1 and v2 were both written before the first measurement. The first real measure-then-fix iteration is v2 → v3. The v1 and v2 flags reproduce the earlier template sets exactly.
