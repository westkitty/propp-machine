# The Tale of Kuzma and Zmei Pickleback

Nobody in a town whose church bell rang a minute early, out of nerves expected much of Kuzma Crookedcap, whose hat pointed always toward trouble; the cat least of all.

When the bees went quiet, the village elder with the brass ear-trumpet packed three onions and a prayer and went away, and the house felt watched.

The village elder with the brass ear-trumpet pinned a note to the door: do not eat the third pancake. Kuzma nodded very seriously, and meant it.

Before the porridge cooled, the house cat forgot, then remembered, then did it on purpose.

Something was missing the shape of the singing apple that told the truth, and the Moonmiller could not sleep for it.

The news went round, and at last it reached the lad.

“Give me a crust and a blessing, and I will go,” said our Kuzma.

With the crows for company, Kuzma walked out of a town whose church bell rang a minute early, out of nerves. At the crossroads stone, the lad took the road down a ladder of roots, deeper than the wells go.

On the way the lad met a hedgehog wearing a bishop’s hat under a cart that had no wheels, who said: “First, help me.”

“Find your own crust,” said the lad. A hedgehog wearing a bishop’s hat said nothing, which was worse.

Following a hare that was not a hare, the lad went down a ladder of roots, deeper than the wells go, until the stars changed at the Copper Kingdom under the earth.

In the very middle of a cave that smelled of brine, our Kuzma stood before Zmei Pickleback. Pickleback challenged our Kuzma to a contest of riddles.

And so Pickleback was defeated.

Our Kuzma found the singing apple that told the truth again.

The lad went home.

At home nobody recognised Kuzma.

A general with polished boots and no battles said that he had done everything.

There was one more task: to find which of twelve identical doves was the princess.

By morning the task was done, with a little left over.

Then the Moonmiller’s daughter recognised our Kuzma.

The lie of a general with polished boots and no battles came out.

And Pickleback: Pickleback was put in a barrel and rolled into the sea, which rolled him back out, ashamed.

So our Kuzma married the Moonmiller’s daughter, with pancakes stacked to the rafters.
