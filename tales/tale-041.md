# The Tale of Kuzma and the Widow of the Well

Nobody in a village where the rooster crowed backwards expected much of Kuzma Crookedcap, whose hat pointed always toward trouble; the neighbours even less.

One day old father Foma went away to market and did not come back for a long time.

Of course Now the Tsar of the Soup Kingdom longed for the water of life and the water of death, and grew thin with longing.

The news went round, and at last it reached Kuzma.

“Give me a crust and a blessing, and I will go,” said our Kuzma.

The lad set out along the road. When the road split like a lizard’s tongue, Kuzma chose the road through the forest, where the trees whispered half of everything.

On the way Kuzma met a ferryman with no shadow at a river that had forgotten its other bank, who said: “First, help me.”

The lad answered with a question and got it right on the third try.

A ferryman with no shadow unfolded a rag that held three things: a horse-hair that summons a horse of storm; a handkerchief that unfolds into a lake; a bone comb that grows forests when thrown. In return Kuzma received a horse-hair that summons a horse of storm.

Following a hare that was not a hare, Kuzma went through the forest, where the trees whispered half of everything, until the stars changed at the Birch Thicket of Unfinished Sentences.

In the very middle of the bottom of the old well, our Kuzma stood before the Widow of the Well. Then, the Widow came out, and they fought.

The Widow was beaten fair, which was the bitter part, and slunk away.

By nobody’s leave but his own, Kuzma recovered the water of life and the water of death.

Then the lad turned back toward a village where the rooster crowed backwards.

Then came a sound of gnawing: the Widow was coming after them.

That same week Our Kuzma burned the horse-hair, and a storm-horse came snorting out of nowhere, and the Widow was stuck behind it for three days.

Kuzma came back in rags, and nobody knew him.

The court demanded proof: to answer a riddle that had no right answer.

By luck and stubbornness, Kuzma did it, and yawned.

By his old crooked grin, they knew Kuzma at last.

Justice in that kingdom was strange but thorough: the Widow was made to count the crows of the kingdom, and is counting still.

So Kuzma married Tsarevna Lumina, with pancakes stacked to the rafters.
