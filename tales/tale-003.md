# The Tale of Yelena and Zmei Pickleback

Past three rivers and a rumour, in a hut beside a river that ran uphill on Sundays, there lived Yelena Thimble-Eyes, who could see a needle think.

At dusk, Zmei Pickleback listened.

The youngest cousin told the stranger everything, and Pickleback smiled with too many teeth.

Then Pickleback came and took Tsarevich Lev Pale-Hands away.

“Who will go?” cried the hero’s grandmother. Only the girl stood up.

Yelena decided to set things right.

Our Yelena set out along the road. Where three ways met, Yelena took the road that went over the sea, where the fish kept diaries and the waves read them aloud.

Under a dripping fir, Yelena met a ferryman with no shadow at a river that had forgotten its other bank. “Carry my bundle a while,” it said, and the trees leaned in to listen.

Not long after, since she had been raised properly, Yelena bowed low.

A ferryman with no shadow unfolded a rag that held three things: a birch-bark flute that makes the dead dance politely; a jar of borrowed thunder; a handkerchief that unfolds into a lake. In return Yelena received a birch-bark flute that makes the dead dance politely.

At last, Yelena stood before Pickleback. Yelena and Pickleback fought in the open field.

With a last blow, Pickleback burst into moths.

Tsarevich Lev Pale-Hands was free.

After that, our Yelena came home by the short road, which is never short.

On the way home, Cousin Smarm the water-carrier pushed Yelena down a well and went home with the credit.

There sat a ferryman with no shadow at a river that had forgotten its other bank, who asked the girl a question.

The girl answered politely.

A ferryman with no shadow opened a sack that held three things: a birch-bark flute that makes the dead dance politely; a jar of borrowed thunder; a handkerchief that unfolds into a lake. From under the moss, a ferryman with no shadow brought a birch-bark flute that makes the dead dance politely, and the air smelled of thunder.

Up out of the dark, Yelena came back into the light, thinner but alive.

Cousin Smarm the water-carrier polished his boots and claimed the deed as his own.

The lie fell over by itself: Cousin Smarm the water-carrier had done nothing at all.

The girl washed and became handsome.

And Pickleback was turned into a draughty doorway that everyone complained about.

There was a wedding, and the bells were rung so hard that one ran away.
