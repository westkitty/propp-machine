# The Tale of Marfa and Zmei Pickleback

Beyond the thrice-ninth land, in a village where the rooster crowed backwards, there was born Marfa Sootfoot, who could whistle in three directions at once.

Then old father Foma set off on business and left Marfa alone.

A very thin stranger came asking what the house was afraid of. It was Zmei Pickleback.

What one hears at a keyhole is soon known in a lair: Pickleback learned exactly enough.

Before long Now the Tsaritsa of Nine Rivers longed for a feather of the Ember-Bird, and grew thin with longing.

So Marfa left home. At the crossroads stone, Marfa took the road down a ladder of roots, deeper than the wells go.

There sat a hedgehog wearing a bishop’s hat under a cart that had no wheels, who asked Marfa a question.

Our Marfa snorted, and walked on.

Now Riding the wind’s spare horse, Marfa went down a ladder of roots, deeper than the wells go, until the stars changed at the Copper Kingdom under the earth.

By the gift’s doing, Marfa recovered a feather of the Ember-Bird.

Then the girl turned back toward a village where the rooster crowed backwards.

The girl came back with a beard of dust, and nobody knew her.

The girl washed and became handsome.

As for Pickleback: Pickleback was turned into a draughty doorway that everyone complained about.

Then there was a wedding.
