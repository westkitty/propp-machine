# The Tale of Kuzma and Grandmother Ninetoes

Nobody in a village where the rooster crowed backwards expected much of Kuzma Crookedcap, whose hat pointed always toward trouble; the neighbours even less.

One day old father Foma went away to market and did not come back for a long time.

“Listen,” said old father Foma, “do not eat the third pancake.” Kuzma crossed his heart, and meant it.

But the youngest cousin forgot the warning at once.

Grandmother Ninetoes came in disguise, holding out a sweet cake.

The lad believed every word.

In the morning there was a cold place where the Moonmiller’s daughter had been. Ninetoes had come.

Of course Old father Foma called Kuzma and said: “Go, and bring it back.”

“I have nothing better to do, and I will go,” said Kuzma.

With the crows for company, Kuzma walked out of a village where the rooster crowed backwards. When the road split like a lizard’s tongue, our Kuzma chose the road that went down a ladder of roots, deeper than the wells go.

By a fire nobody had lit, Kuzma met a hedgehog wearing a bishop’s hat under a cart that had no wheels. “Carry my bundle a while,” it said, and the trees leaned in to listen.

Since he had been raised properly, the lad said thank you to the trees.

A hedgehog wearing a bishop’s hat unfolded a rag that held three things: a ball of red thread that knows the way; a handkerchief that unfolds into a lake; a horse-hair that summons a horse of storm. A hedgehog wearing a bishop’s hat coughed up, rather damply a handkerchief that unfolds into a lake. “Do not thank me yet.”

On the back of the judge-goose, Kuzma went down a ladder of roots, deeper than the wells go, until the stars changed at the Copper Kingdom under the earth.

Then, everything that had been taken was put right.

Cousin Smarm the water-carrier said that he had done everything.

One more trial was set: to sit in a red-hot bathhouse and come out cold.

With help from the judge-goose, the task was done.

Everyone recognised our Kuzma at last.

The lie of Cousin Smarm the water-carrier came out.

Kuzma stepped through the judge-goose’s ear and out the other and came out handsome.

Ninetoes was put in a barrel and rolled into the sea, which rolled her back out, ashamed.

Then there was a wedding.
